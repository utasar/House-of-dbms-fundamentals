why generalize ?
You are taking two existing, lower-level entity sets (StandardMember and PremiumMember) that share common attributes and synthesizing them into a higher-level superclass (Member). Specialization works in reverse—starting with a top-level entity and breaking it down into specific subtypes.

identify:
Superclass: Member

Subclasses: StandardMember, PremiumMember

Shared Attributes (Superclass): MemberID (Primary Key), Name, Email, JoinDate

Subclass-Specific Attribute: MonthlyTrainingHours (belongs exclusively to PremiumMember)


Hierarchy Constraints
Disjointness: Disjoint

Justification: The problem specifies that "every member has exactly one membership type: standard or premium." A member cannot belong to both subclasses simultaneously.



challenge:
If the gym wants to explicitly track day-pass members, you could introduce a third subclass (DayPassMember). Doing so would maintain a total specialization constraint, but it would have no unique subclass attributes of its own.
